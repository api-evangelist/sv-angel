SV Angel Founder Profile Series: Monica Sarbu — Xata For the May 2022 SV Angel Founder Profile Series, we are proud to feature Monica Sarbu , Founder and CEO of Xata . Xata offers a serverless database service that combines the power of a traditional database with the feel of a spreadsheet. The company recently announced their $30M Series A financing and an all-women board of directors , including the former COO of SV Angel portfolio company GitHub .
