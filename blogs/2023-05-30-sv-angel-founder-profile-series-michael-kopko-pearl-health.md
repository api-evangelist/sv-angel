SV Angel Founder Profile Series: Michael Kopko — Pearl Health We are proud to feature Michael Kopko , Co-Founder and CEO of Pearl Health for the May 2023 SV Angel Founder Profile Series. Pearl Health is democratizing access to value in healthcare by offering software and services that help providers reimagine how they visualize, understand, and care for their patients. Over the past year, more than 800 primary care providers across the country partnered with Pearl Health, which resulted in more than 10x year-over-year growth.
